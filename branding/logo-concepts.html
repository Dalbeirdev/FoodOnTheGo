FoodOnTheGo — Logo Concepts (Draft 2)

Modern direction: bold geometric marks, a warm orange-to-coral gradient, rounded type. Each one is shown on light and dark backgrounds, as an Android app icon, and on a splash screen.

01

Chef Pin

A location pin with a chef's hat inside. It says "a chef's food, waiting at a stop on your route" in one simple shape. It's bold, easy to recognise at any size, and the pin can be reused as the restaurant marker on the map. My recommendation.

Go-Fork

A "G" for Go, drawn as a single continuous route that ends in a fork. It's a clean, tech-style monogram in the spirit of modern delivery and travel apps, and works well as a small icon or favicon.

Chef Dash
