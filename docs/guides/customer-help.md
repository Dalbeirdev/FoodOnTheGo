# FoodOnTheGo — customer help (FAQ)

The source text for the Help & Support content of Customer Web and the Android app. Every answer describes what the
platform actually does (Module 41 review). Wording that depends on decisions still pending (legal policies, support
contact, cancellation) is marked **pending** and must not be published until approved.

## Ordering

**What is FoodOnTheGo?** Order food from restaurants along your route and pick it up at the counter — no delivery.

**How do I find restaurants on my way?** Open **Plan a Journey**, enter where you start and where you are going (or use
your current location — only when you tap it). FoodOnTheGo shows restaurants close to your route, how far off the route
they are and whether they are open when you arrive.

**Can I browse without an account?** Yes: journeys, restaurants, menus and your cart work as a guest. You sign in with
your phone number (one-time code) before checkout; your guest cart comes with you.

**Can I order from two restaurants at once?** No — one restaurant per order. Adding an item from another restaurant asks
whether to start a new cart.

**When do I pick up?** Choose **ASAP** or a scheduled time the restaurant offers. With a journey, FoodOnTheGo recommends
a time that matches your arrival. Times are the restaurant's local time.

**Why did my price change?** Prices are always the restaurant's current prices, checked by FoodOnTheGo when you add,
when you open your cart and at checkout. If something changed or sold out, the cart tells you before you pay.

## Payment

**How do I pay?** Online through our payment provider (Razorpay): UPI, cards, net banking or wallets as offered.
FoodOnTheGo never sees or stores your card number, CVV or UPI PIN.

**Can I pay cash at pickup?** No. Orders are paid online.

**My payment went through but I see "processing".** Do not pay again. We confirm the payment with the provider; your
order appears as soon as it is confirmed. If it does not, open a support case — you will not be charged twice.

**Is there a delivery or convenience fee?** No delivery fee (we do not deliver). Taxes are shown as configured for the
restaurant (**pending**: approved tax rules).

## Your order

**How do I follow my order?** **My Orders** → the order: accepted, preparing, ready — it updates live, and you get a
notification when it changes (push on Android when notifications are allowed).

**How do I collect it?** At the counter, show the **QR code** or the **pickup code** from your order screen. The code
works once, only for that order, only when it is ready. Never share it with anyone you do not want to collect your food.

**What if the restaurant rejects my order?** You are owed a full refund to your payment method; FoodOnTheGo processes it
(whether this happens without a manual approval step is **pending** — refund policy CF-476).

**Can I cancel?** **Pending** — customer cancellation is not available yet; open a support case.

**Refunds — how long?** Refunds go back to the original payment method; the bank or provider timing applies. We show
"refunded" only when the provider confirms it.

**Reviews:** after you picked up an order you can review it once (Orders → Review).

## Account

**Saved places** are shortcuts for planning journeys (home, work, a station) — not delivery addresses.

**Favorites** keep restaurants you like; they sync between the web and the app.

**Notifications:** choose what you receive per channel under **Notifications**; order updates in the app's inbox are
always kept.

**Change my phone number:** Profile → change number — a code is sent to the new number; other devices are signed out.

**Delete my account:** Profile → delete account. Your request is recorded and your account is restricted; erasure
follows the retention rules (**pending** final approval).

**Signed out unexpectedly?** Sessions expire for your security; sign in again with a new code.
